# Long-Duration Grid Energy Storage (2025 Analysis)
**Source ID:** ENERGY-GRID-2025

## 1. Iron-Air Batteries
Iron-Air (Fe-Air) batteries are designed for multi-day long-duration energy storage:
- Operating Principle: Metallic iron (Fe) oxidizes in alkaline electrolyte (KOH) with ambient atmospheric oxygen (O2) during discharge (reversible rusting). Charging reduces rust back to metallic iron.
- Cost: Levelized capital storage cost of approximately $20/kWh—less than one-tenth the cost of lithium-ion systems.
- Efficiency: 45-50% round-trip efficiency over 100-hour continuous discharge cycles.

## 2. Vanadium Redox Flow Batteries (VRFB)
For intermediate-duration storage (6-16 hours):
- Decoupled Power & Energy: Energy capacity is determined by vanadium liquid electrolyte tank volume, while power output (MW) depends on membrane stack surface area.
- Lifespan: Zero degradation over 25,000 charge-discharge cycles (over 30-year operational life) because the electroactive species remains dissolved in liquid without solid-state phase transitions.
- Efficiency: 70-78% round-trip efficiency at 10°C to 40°C.
